Process

How CivicReport Works

From spotting a problem to seeing it fixed — four simple steps to a better city.

👁 Step 1

Spot an Issue

Notice a pothole, broken light, or any public infrastructure problem in your area.

📝 Step 2

Submit a Report

Open the app, snap a photo, pinpoint the location, and describe the issue. Done in under a minute.

📨 Step 3

We Forward It

Your report is automatically routed to the responsible government institution or municipal department.

✅ Step 4

Track Progress

Receive real-time updates as the issue moves through review, assignment, and resolution.
