System Capabilities

WHAT IT DOES

[01] ☎

INSTANT REPORTING

Snap a photo, drop a pin, file a report. No bureaucracy. No forms in triplicate. Direct line to the people in charge.

[02] ⌖

GPS PRECISION

Exact location tracking pins your report to the meter. No ambiguity. No excuses for institutions to claim they cannot find the problem.

[03] ≡

LIVE TRACKING

Watch your report move through the system in real time. Every status change logged. Full transparency, zero opacity.

[04] ♣

INSTITUTION ROUTING

Reports automatically route to the correct government department. No more "that's not our department" runaround.

[05] ♠

COMMUNITY VOTING

Upvote critical issues. When the community speaks together, reports get prioritized. Numbers create pressure.

[06] ♦

PUBLIC DATA

All reports are public record. Open data for journalists, researchers, and citizens who demand accountability from their government.

HOW IT WORKS

01
[STEP ONE]

SPOT IT

→
02
[STEP TWO]

REPORT IT

→
03
[STEP THREE]

→
